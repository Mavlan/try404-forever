Forever Simple World Map 0.30.2 by Tarolg, identified in upstream's notice as
MIT. The MIT notice is retained in THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt. Upstream says it
had the author's agreement; that is not a claim of separate permission to us.
The newer separately licensed Blackfathom Deeps room layout is not included.
The room images referred to are game-client minimap assets. Some boss pins
are attributed upstream to foreverchanges.pro; their reuse terms are not
independently verified. The three ls_*.tga images originate in the upstream
6.2.10/6.2.11 baseline; their independent provenance remains to be checked.

We excluded 111 legacy dungeon images carried over unchanged from OneForAll
6.2.9: the current DUNGEON_ART table is empty and 6.2.11 uses client loading
screen assets or three ls_*.tga files instead. Game-client assets remain the
property of their respective rightsholders; this notice grants no rights to
those materials. Other third-party sources retain their separate terms.
