Seal rank spell IDs
  The seal spell IDs in UnsealedWrathTracker.lua (SEAL_RANKS) come from the

  (https://talentsforever.com/data.json), licensed under Creative Commons
  Attribution 4.0 International (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/),
  as compiled in the addon "Pally Seal Cooldowns" by Colby.
  Changes: the IDs are grouped by seal and used to recognize seal casts and to
  filter Blizzard's native aura display. No endorsement by talentsforever.com,
  Colby or Blizzard is implied.
